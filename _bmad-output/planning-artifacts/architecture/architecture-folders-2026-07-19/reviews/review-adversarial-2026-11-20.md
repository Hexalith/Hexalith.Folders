# Adversarial review of the Folders architecture

20 November 2026 · Whole-record validation · Implementer audience

**Verdict: FAIL.** The provider failure rules admit a second write after an ambiguous result. The context-read recipe and idempotency rules also leave room for implementations with different authorization and replay behavior. Each finding below compares two readings of the current document against an existing authority. Open provider qualification, deployment and release evidence are outside this finding count.

Another records the same attempt as unknown and enters reconciliation. The first adapter can duplicate a write accepted before the timeout. | Provider compatibility catalog lines 128 and 136 distinguish cancellation before dispatch from ambiguous outcomes after dispatch. PRD line 874 and the November 20 audit require read-only reconciliation for the latter. | Define the branch by dispatch and confirmation evidence.
