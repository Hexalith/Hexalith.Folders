# A4 / PD5 Decision Payload

Version: `1.0.0-candidate.1`

Rename Story 10.9 to “Preserve Metadata-Only Indexing Until C9 Body-Content Approval.” Metadata-only search is the MVP behavior. Story 10.9 completes only by proving that unauthorized or unapproved body content cannot be indexed, hydrated, or returned. It is not a forward capability dependency. Any future body-content indexing capability requires a new stable requirement, a new story, an execution rank, exact prerequisite edges, and C9-backed Product, Security, and Architecture approval.
