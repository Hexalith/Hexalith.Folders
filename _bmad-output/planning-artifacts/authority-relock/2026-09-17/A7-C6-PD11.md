# A7 / C6 / PD11 Decision Payload

Version: `1.0.0-candidate.1`

Adopt one total `(state, event, guard)` lifecycle model across architecture, C6 mapping, lifecycle diagram, code, generated v2 enum, UI disposition mapping, and tests. Every omitted triple rejects with `state_transition_invalid`, leaves state unchanged, and records an inspectable idempotent rejection. These five pairs are guard-discriminated and may never fall through to a sibling branch:

1. `unknown_provider_outcome + ReconciliationCompletedDirty`: confirmed commit goes to `committed`; confirmed refusal goes to `failed`.
2. `changes_staged + CommitFailed`: retryable with no confirmed remote effect goes to `dirty` with staged changes preserved; known non-retryable goes to `failed`.
3. `inaccessible + ProviderReadinessValidated`: staged content present before `stagedRecoveryDeadline` goes to `dirty`; no staged content goes to `ready`; staged content after the recovery deadline rejects until separate C3 cleanup completes.
4. `dirty + WorkspaceLocked`: staged content plus equality between `stagedByTaskId` and the server-resolved authorized task goes to `changes_staged`; every other branch rejects.
5. `dirty + LockLeaseBecameStale`: a clean dirty workspace with no staged content goes to `ready` and `unlocked`; staged content rejects.

Durable state includes `stagedByTaskId`, `stagedByPrincipal`, `stagedRecoveryStartedAt`, and `stagedRecoveryDeadline`; the cleanup workflow separately records `stagedCleanupStartedAt` and `stagedCleanupNotBefore`. Task binding is server-resolved. `unknown_provider_outcome` is `auto-recovering` during at most five read-only checks within fifteen minutes and becomes `reconciliation_required`/`awaiting-human` only when those checks cannot establish the result.

`OperatorDiscardRequested`, `OperatorRetrySucceeded`, and `OperatorMarkedFailed` are reserved post-MVP identifiers: they are absent from the published MVP enum and active diagram, reject in MVP code, preserve state, and are negatively tested. `LockLeaseBecameStale` is added to the enum and is the only event that moves a lock from expired to stale at the inclusive C7 boundary.
