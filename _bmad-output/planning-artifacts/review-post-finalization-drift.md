## Verdict

The triage closed all four High findings of the previous pass in the direction of the approved artifacts: the ACL verb table became a "Protected operation families and permissions" table mapped onto the Spine's `FolderPermissionLevel`; FR44 became an outcome-family enumeration realized by the Spine under a mapping owed as C13 evidence, with the leaking categories recorded as conformance defects in PD10; the lease-lapse row now follows the approved C6 matrix (`locked` + lease expiry → `dirty`); A3/A4 were withdrawn for the approved OQ8 design; PD4 was widened and PD5 refreshed. Programmatic checks confirm the 58 `- FRn:` clause lines and the 73 NFR bullets in `epics.md` are still byte-identical to the PRD, every unmarked evidence path exists and every "(to be created)" path is absent, and every Spine identifier the PRD now claims (`X-Hexalith-Task-Id`, `FolderPermissionLevel` = `read|write|administer`, the `SafeAuthorizationDenial` envelopes, `releaseReasonCode` = `caller_completed|caller_abandoned|operator_requested|authorization_revoked|task_cancelled|lock_revoked`, all 24 illustrative operation ids, `LifecycleState`/`LockState` values, `lockOwnershipProof`, `isTruncated`, `idempotency_key_expired`) is present. The PRD therefore holds as product authority. Two High items remain, both created or left uncovered by the triage: (1) the completion table still carries four transitions that the approved C6 matrix and the coded `FolderStateTransitions` do not have (revocation with staged changes → `inaccessible`, `inaccessible` → `dirty` on restored authority, originating-task resumption out of `dirty`, and commit failure/ref conflict → `dirty` where the matrix says `failed`) while the C6 status cell now claims the vocabularies are shared and records only the disposition/discard disagreement; (2) the safe-denial invariant demands one status per operation, but the Spine declares two status-distinct safe-denial envelopes (403 and 404) on the same operations with overlapping case lists, and PD10 does not list that conflict. No finding is Critical; two are High, four Medium, five Low.
