critical

UX-DR36 collapsed two envelopes that architecture S-7 separates

UX-DR36 and Non-Disclosing Unavailability Envelopes
S-7 mandates two envelopes: HTTP 404 authorization/tenant_access_denied (retryable:false) for a post-authorization denial, and HTTP 503 availability/authority_unavailable (retryable:true, details.visibility:redacted) for authority absent, stale, malformed or unavailable. Its stated rationale is removing the existence oracle "without hiding a real outage behind it". The collapsed form would render a genuine outage as a denial and suppress the retry the operator needs.
Fixed: UX-DR36 rewritten and the section replaced with a two-envelope table carrying each envelope's status, category, code, retryability and the operator's next move.
