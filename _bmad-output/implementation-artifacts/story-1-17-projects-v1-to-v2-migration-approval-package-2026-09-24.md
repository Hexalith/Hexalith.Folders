Stop immediately for an authorization or disclosure regression, failed Projects smoke, unexplained v2 errors, loss of route attribution, breach of the agreed error/latency thresholds, an unplanned v1 consumer, or inability to meet the deadline. For an ordinary rollback, Delivery keeps the Folders v1 route working, restores the prior Projects v1 deployment, verifies lifecycle, effective-permissions, and file-metadata calls through v1, then disables v2 exposure. For an active authorization or disclosure incident, disable v2 exposure immediately, restore Projects v1, and verify v1 calls. Record both deployment/configuration IDs, T0, trigger, owner, timestamps, and metadata-only outcomes. Review any disclosure or authorization event before further exposure. Do not claim completed v2 mutations are reversed.

If T0 + 168 hours arrives without complete exit evidence and an approved retirement cutover, stop the exception and escalate for a new explicit decision; do not silently extend, retire v1, or call the migration complete.
